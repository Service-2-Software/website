Path to Approval

The SkillBridge timeline is predictable when you know what's coming. Here's how to move from eligible to hired.

01

Confirm SkillBridge Eligibility

You need at least 180 days of active duty service remaining before ETS/retirement. Contact your chain of command to begin the approval process. S2S provides a unit commander letter template to speed up authorization.

02

Apply to S2S

Submit your application below. Our team reviews every application within 48 hours. We're looking for service members with 4+ years of experience, strong communication skills, and genuine interest in technology sales.

03

Complete Intro Assessment

A 30-minute intro call with our team to assess fit and goals. No sales experience required. We're evaluating communication, coachability, and mission alignment — skills you've been building for years.

04

SDR School — Weeks 1–4

Intensive, live sales training covering prospecting, discovery, objection handling, CRM workflows, and Salesforce Agentblazer AI certification. You're a professional before you ever hit a quota.

05

Live Partner Internship — Months 2–6

Embedded with a hiring partner company as a Sales Development Rep. Real pipeline, real quota, real mentorship — with S2S coaching throughout. 70% of interns receive a full-time offer before program end.

06

Placed and Producing

Hired as a full-time employee — typically 30 to 60 days before your ETS date. Zero gap between service and your civilian career. Average first-year OTE: $80–90K.
