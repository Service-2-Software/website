FAQ

Common Questions

Is there any cost to service members?

No. The program is SkillBridge eligible and there is no cost to transitioning service members.

How long is the program?

It runs inside your SkillBridge window, typically the final months before separation.

What is the headcount commitment for companies?

Zero added headcount burden during ramp. Sellers contribute during SkillBridge before any full-time decision.

What kind of roles do graduates land?

SDR, BDR, and Account Executive roles across high-growth software companies.
