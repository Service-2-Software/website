Partner Process

How It Works

Three steps from partnership agreement to your first S2S hire producing pipeline.

Step 01

Identify & Vet

S2S matches your role requirements to candidates in the active cohort. We present only pre-vetted, sales-trained veterans who fit your company's ICP, culture, and territory needs.

Step 02

Embed & Train

The candidate joins your team as a SkillBridge intern. S2S handles ongoing coaching, performance reviews, and support. Your team runs standard onboarding — we handle the rest.

Step 03

Hire & Build

At program end, make your hire decision with 6 months of performance data in hand. 70% of S2S partners convert their intern to a full-time employee — and most expand to 2+ hires.
