Our Story

Why We Built S2S

Too many transitioning service members leave the military with elite leadership skills and no clear path into a career that values them. Too many sales teams burn budget on hires that take most of a year to ramp. S2S closes both gaps at once, training veterans for modern sales and placing them with companies that need ramp-ready talent.

Selling, done right, is not persuasion. It's purpose. It's understanding a problem deeply enough to solve it, and caring enough to follow through. That's the same instinct that drives service. We just point it at a new mission.
