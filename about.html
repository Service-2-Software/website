About Service 2 Software

BUILT ON
SERVICE.
DRIVEN BY PURPOSE.

S2S exists because military service members deserve more than a thank-you. They deserve a career that honors what they've built — and a pathway that makes it real.

Core Belief System

Sales Is the Bridge Between Service and Success

Sales is not about manipulation or pressure. It is about connection, service, and solving real problems for real people. Military professionals already know this — they just haven't been told it applies to the boardroom.

At Service 2 Software, we believe the same attributes that create elite soldiers create elite salespeople: mission focus, resilience under pressure, tactical thinking, and an uncompromising commitment to the team.

"Not persuasion, but purpose."

Our Origin

Careers are built through relationships, not résumés. S2S was founded to close the gap between military greatness and civilian opportunity.

How We Operate

Guiding Principles

These are the beliefs that shape every decision we make — from curriculum design to candidate selection to partner relationships.

01

Mission Over Metrics

We measure success by the quality of transitions and the longevity of careers, not volume of placements. Every candidate gets the full mission because every life matters.

02

Character First

Skills can be taught. Character is built over years of service. We select for integrity, grit, and coachability — and trust the training to take care of the rest.

03

Radical Accountability

We hold our candidates to standards. We hold our partners to standards. And we hold ourselves to even higher ones. Accountability is not punishment — it's the foundation of trust.

04

Earned, Not Given

We don't hand out placements. Candidates earn their spot through performance, coachability, and commitment. Partners earn access to top-tier talent by investing in the relationship.

05

Service Beyond Self

Every S2S team member served or understands service. We built this program because we believe the country's obligation to those who served doesn't end at the gate. It starts there.

06

Purpose as Competitive Advantage

Companies that hire with purpose — deliberately, thoughtfully, for fit and mission — outperform those that hire reactively. S2S gives you the structure to do it right.

Common Questions

FAQ

Is S2S really free for candidates?

Yes. The entire program — training, coaching, placement — is fully funded. Candidates are compensated through the DOD SkillBridge program while they complete the internship.

Do companies pay for S2S candidates?

There is a partnership structure. Contact us for specifics. Compared to traditional recruiting and training costs, S2S partners consistently see 8x+ ROI in year one.

What branches of service are eligible?

All branches — Army, Navy, Marines, Air Force, Space Force, Coast Guard, National Guard, and Reserves — are eligible if the candidate meets the 180-day minimum active service requirement.

How long is the program?

The program runs 6 to 8 months total: 4 weeks of SDR School followed by a 4–6 month embedded internship with a hiring partner company.

What is the employment rate after graduation?

96% of S2S graduates are employed in technology sales within 90 days of separation. 70% receive a full-time offer from their internship host company before the program ends.

Is S2S a nonprofit?

Yes. Service 2 Software is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. Our mission is impact over profit — every dollar goes directly into the program, the candidates, and the mission.
