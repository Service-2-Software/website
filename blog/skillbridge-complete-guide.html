SkillBridge

The Complete SkillBridge Guide
for Military Professionals

8 min read Updated 2025 By S2S Staff

The DOD SkillBridge program is one of the most valuable and underutilized transition tools available to separating and retiring service members. When used strategically, it can mean the difference between a 60-day job search after separation and walking into a full-time offer on your last day in uniform.

This guide covers everything — from eligibility requirements to choosing the right program to what happens during the internship. By the end, you'll have a clear picture of whether SkillBridge is right for you and how to use it to launch a technology sales career.

What Is SkillBridge?

SkillBridge is a Department of Defense program that allows active duty service members to participate in civilian job training, apprenticeships, or internships during their last 180 days of service. The DOD continues to pay your salary and benefits during the program — you essentially work full-time at a civilian company on the government's dime while you figure out your next move.

For tech sales specifically, this means you can complete a full SDR training program and a real-world internship with a tech company before your ETS date — arriving at separation already employed, trained, and producing.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Active duty service member with at least 180 days remaining before separation or retirement
  • All branches qualify: Army, Navy, Marines, Air Force, Space Force, Coast Guard
  • National Guard and Reserve members on active duty orders may also be eligible
  • Commanding officer approval required (S2S provides a template letter)
  • No minimum rank requirement — E-4 through O-6 have all gone through S2S

The Timeline: When to Start

The biggest mistake transitioning service members make with SkillBridge is starting too late. Here's the ideal timeline:

  • 12 months out: Research programs and apply. The best SkillBridge programs (including S2S) have competitive application processes and limited cohort sizes.
  • 9 months out: Receive program acceptance and begin the command approval process. Some commands take 60–90 days to process SkillBridge requests.
  • 6 months out: Begin your SkillBridge internship. This gives you the maximum program duration allowed.
  • 30–60 days before ETS: Receive and accept a full-time job offer from your internship company.

How S2S Uses SkillBridge

Service 2 Software is a DOD-approved SkillBridge partner. Our program is specifically designed to maximize the SkillBridge window for candidates pursuing technology sales careers:

  • Weeks 1–4: SDR School — intensive live sales training, Salesforce certification, CRM workflows, prospecting methodology, and roleplay-heavy coaching
  • Months 2–6: Live partner internship embedded with a hiring partner company as a real SDR with real quota and real coaching

The result: 96% of S2S graduates are employed in technology sales within 90 days of separation, and 70% receive a full-time offer before their program ends.

Choosing the Right SkillBridge Program

Not all SkillBridge programs are equal. Before committing to any program, ask:

  • What is the employment rate for graduates of this program?
  • Does the program include actual training, or just job shadowing?
  • Are there real companies partnered with the program who hire graduates?
  • What is the compensation potential post-graduation?
  • Is the program DOD-approved, or am I just doing unpaid work?

S2S publishes its employment rate (96%), average starting compensation ($80–90K OTE), and partner company list publicly. We encourage you to compare any program on those metrics before applying.

What Happens After SkillBridge?

If you've completed a quality SkillBridge program and performed well during your internship, you should have a full-time offer waiting at separation. The S2S model is specifically built to engineer this outcome — not to hope for it.

After graduating from S2S:

  • 70% are hired by their internship company as full-time employees
  • 26% are placed with other S2S partner companies through our network
  • 96% are employed within 90 days of separation
  • Average starting OTE: $80–90K
  • Access to the S2S alumni network of 600+ graduates in tech sales roles
