DoD SkillBridge lets companies bring on transitioning service members for hands-on experience during their final months of service. Done right, it is the lowest-risk way to add high-performing sales talent. Here is the playbook.

Why SkillBridge works for sales teams

Service members arrive with discipline, coachability, and a bias for action, the traits that separate top sellers from the pack. SkillBridge gives you a window to see that in action before making a full-time decision.
