# Pre-Core Candidate Portal — Create account & log in

Portal: https://s2score.service2software.org/candidatelogin

Use this portal while you are still in the Service2Software interview process (after your initial call, through assessment and interviews).

## Create your account

1. Open **Create account**.
2. Enter the **same email address** used for your initial call with S2S.
3. Choose a password (at least 8 characters).
4. Click **Create Account**.

You will land on your Pre-Core dashboard, where you can track stages and to-dos.

## If registration fails

| Message you may see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| We couldn't find your application | Confirm you used the email from your initial call, or contact your recruiter. |
| Self-registration is only available right after your initial call | Your Lead status may have already moved forward — ask your recruiter to help you get access. |
| An account already exists | Go to Sign in or reset your password. |

## Log in

1. Open https://s2score.service2software.org/candidatelogin.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Click **Sign In**.

You will go to your Pre-Core home: https://s2score.service2software.org/candidates/leads.

## Forgot password

On the login page, click **Forgot password?**  
Or go directly to https://s2score.service2software.org/candidatelogin/reset.

Follow the steps to reset your password with the email for your account.

## After you’re accepted

Once you are accepted into the program (acceptance letter / Salesforce conversion), you will use the **S2S Core** portal instead:

- Login: https://s2score.service2software.org/login

See the separate S2S Core candidate guide for register and login steps.

## Need help?

Contact your S2S recruiter or program contact if you cannot register or sign in with the email from your initial call.
