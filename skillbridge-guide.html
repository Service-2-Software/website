The Complete Guide to DoD SkillBridge

DoD SkillBridge lets transitioning service members spend their final months on active duty training for a civilian career, while still drawing military pay and benefits. It's one of the most valuable transition tools available, and most people leave it on the table.

What SkillBridge Actually Is

SkillBridge is a Department of Defense authorization that allows you to work and train with an approved industry partner during your last 180 days of service. Your command grants the time; the partner provides the training and the on-ramp to a job.

Who Qualifies

Most service members within 180 days of separation are eligible with command approval. The exact window and requirements vary by branch and command, so start the conversation early, ideally six to eight months out.
